## Early-Stage Research

### Exploring pedestrian movement through colour for everyone

- Author: Nicholas R. Harding
- Repository: University of Canterbury Research Repository
- Link: <https://ir.canterbury.ac.nz/items/0816750d-de14-4fce-82f1-9a42bf4b68ae>

This thesis examined how pedestrian movement visualisations can be made more
accessible for people with colour vision deficiency. The work used crowd
simulation outputs, BIM-authored building geometry, and VR-based evaluation to
study alternatives to conventional colour-dependent heatmaps.

The topic aligns closely with Project Mobius because the project also focuses
on 3D pedestrian replay, heatmap-style crowd analysis, and communicating crowd
behaviour clearly in an interactive spatial environment. It is a useful
reference for the accessibility and visualisation goals that shaped the early
direction of the project.
